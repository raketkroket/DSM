# Temporary photography — sources and licences

All photographs in `public/images/stock/` are **temporary** placeholders until Dedicated Security Management B.V.
supplies its own photography. They are self-hosted (downloaded, resized to max. 2600 px, metadata stripped) and served
through `next/image` as AVIF/WebP. Nothing is hotlinked.

**Licences.** Every image is free for commercial use under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) or the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (verified per photo page on 2026-10-26; none are Unsplash+). Attribution is
not required; credits are kept below anyway.

**Restrictions observed.** Neither licence includes model or property releases. Therefore:

- No image is captioned or described as DSM staff, a DSM client or a DSM location. The site footer states this
  automatically for as long as any stock photo is published.
- Images with visible weapons, company logos or brands, real street addresses or masks were rejected.
- Photos must not be sold or redistributed as standalone files.

**Replacing them.** Admin → *Foto’s vervangen* lists every image slot that still shows a stock photo, with one-click upload.
Files under `/images/stock/` are flagged as temporary automatically.
